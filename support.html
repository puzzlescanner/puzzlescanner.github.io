Support

Puzzle Scanner

Something not working, or an idea? In the game: Settings → Send feedback. Or write to puzzlescanner.support@gmail.com.

Questions

I bought "Remove ads" - how do I get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling?
Shop → Restore purchases (on iPhone). On Android it comes back by itself. Use the same Apple ID / Google account you bought it with. The coins and Clear 100s that came with it are given once, when it is bought.

Where is my progress saved?
On your phone. Levels, the daily streak, coins and your artworks stay on the device; deleting the app deletes them.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. Photo puzzles are made on your phone, and the cut-out is your phone's own (Apple's or Google's, on the device). Only the puzzle's cells are kept, never the photo.

Ads
A full-screen ad shows now and then between puzzles; "Remove ads" in the Shop turns those off for good. Ads you choose to watch for a reward (coins, hearts) stay available. In the EU and UK, you can change your ad choices in Settings.
