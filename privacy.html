Privacy policy

Puzzle Scanner: Arrow Puzzle · Last updated: October 2026

Puzzle Scanner is made by one independent developer in Vietnam ("I", "me"). This page says plainly what the game collects, why, and who else handles it. Questions or requests: puzzlescanner.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels, the daily streak, coins, credits), your settings and your artworks are saved only on your device. I never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Your photos never leave your phone. Photo puzzles are made on the device, and the cut-out uses your phone's own built-in feature (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google ML Kit on Android), which also runs on the device. Only the puzzle's cells are kept, never the photo.

What is collected, and why

Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): which puzzles are started, finished or left, how long they take, when an ad or a purchase happens, settings changes, plus the app version, phone model, operating system, and an approximate country from the network. Used to see where the game is too hard or too easy and to fix it. No name, no email, no photos.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the game crashes, what went wrong, the phone model and system version. Used to fix bugs.

Feedback you send (Settings → Send feedback, stored in Google's Cloud Firestore): the text you write, with your phone's system (iOS or Android, and its version). Nothing else - no name or account. Please don't write personal details in it.

Ads (Google AdMob): to show ads and count them, Google receives information such as your IP address, phone model, and - on Android - the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in Android's settings. On iPhone the game does not ask to track you and does not use Apple's advertising identifier. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks first whether ads may be personalised; you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google's own policy: how Google uses information from apps.

Purchases ("Remove ads") are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. I never see your payment details; the store only tells the game that the item was bought.

Who handles the data

Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Firestore, AdMob) and Apple / Google for purchases - each under its own privacy policy. I don't sell your data or share it with anyone else.

How long it is kept

Gameplay statistics: 14 months. Crash reports: 90 days. Feedback: until it has been read and dealt with, at most 2 years.

Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can change ad consent in Settings (EU, UK, Switzerland), reset the advertising ID on Android, and stop all collection by deleting the app. Wherever you live - and under the GDPR in the EU and UK - you can ask what is held about you, or ask for it to be deleted, by writing to puzzlescanner.support@gmail.com. As the statistics carry no name, it helps to say roughly when you sent any feedback.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
